## The lead: FA chair Hewitt demands Infantino release World Cup sell-off papers
FA chair and Fifa vice-president Debbie Hewitt has written to Gianni Infantino demanding he release all documents on his plan to sell a stake in the World Cup, and calling for an independent review of how that scrapped proposal was conceived and tendered. The letter also seeks a written explanation for the cancelled one-match suspension of USA forward Folarin Balogun, and asks for documents before the Fifa Council meets on October 15. It was also sent to secretary general Mattias Grafstrom and accuses Fifa of governance failures. The scrapped Fifa Forward Enterprise plan would have sold 21% of a new commercial and ticketing rights company to a private investor; Uefa has separately begun preparing US legal proceedings over it. England, Wales and Scotland have withdrawn support for Infantino's re-election bid, though Africa, South America and Oceania's confederations still back him. Infantino, standing for a fourth term in March, must face Hewitt and the other 35 Fifa Council members next month.
