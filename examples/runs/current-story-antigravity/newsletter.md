## The lead: Real Madrid Keen on Manchester United 15-Year-Old Forward JJ Gabriel
Real Madrid have a strong interest in signing 15-year-old Manchester United forward JJ Gabriel, who has sent Manchester United formal notice that he wants to cancel his registration with immediate effect. Real Madrid want to recruit Gabriel immediately and position him in their reserve team, Real Madrid Castilla. Multiple major European clubs, including Barcelona, PSG, Bayern Munich, and Chelsea, also have a firm interest in signing Gabriel.
