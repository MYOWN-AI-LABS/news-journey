Try your first two editions.

Choose your sources, create a newsletter and video, and review them in your own workspace. Publishing goes only to the channels you connect, after your review. Pro will be offered separately.

Your first two editions

  1. Describe your audience, publication and trusted sources.
  2. Choose your own writing provider. Local-to-hosted rescue starts off. Optional local voice and rendering dependencies require setup.
  3. Continue with Free neutral defaults. Optional topic controls and planned Pro choices stay collapsed until you open them. Review the proposed stories and visuals.
  4. Create a private preview. Open the newsletter and video. Check factual claims, source links, readability, pronunciation and length.
  5. Reload: the same result should reopen. Create a second edition and record the time and assistance needed.

No Pro checkout, private signing tool, premium pack or hosted service is included. MIT permits modification of the source; these are supported distribution and service boundaries.

A portable source package

The archive contains source and dependency manifests, with no installed driver binaries, model weights, private browser profiles, credentials or developer workspace. Connect your own API provider or local model. The app installs its usual dependencies; video generation also needs rendering and narration tools.

What has to work

npm test checks this Free package's private server, authorization, neutral defaults, Pro refusals, and that no publishing call leaves your computer during the checks. npm run typecheck checks the extracted source. npm run dry-run uses a synthetic fixture. None is a real-model, voice or publication-quality pass.

Real-model length, facts, visuals and narration still need your own review. Hosted executive access and commercial services are separate, unfinished work.
