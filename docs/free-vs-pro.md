# Free and planned Pro

| Capability | Free (this package) | Planned Pro release |
|---|---|---|
| Writing and sources | Your own provider, sources and editorial vocabulary | Managed workflow and hosting options |
| Newsletter/video | Neutral defaults; story choices; private generated artifacts | Supported brand, style, length and presenter personalization |
| Narration | Built-in narrator and optional local voice using your own installed dependencies | Hosted voice and managed presenters, subject to consent and metering |
| Review | Exact-artifact review and access | Same review requirements |
| Publishing | Publish to the channels you connect, after your review | Managed delivery services planned; no weaker approval rules |
| Operational receipts | Model usage, job progress and delivery status | Same plus managed audience analytics |
| LinkedIn writing | Nine drafting/review tasks; optional bounded public-post reading with your own Apify account; manual copy/download | Managed audience workflows and reviewed outreach |
| Audience analytics/outreach | Unavailable through this distribution's runtime entry points | Planned managed analytics and reviewed outreach |
| Logo and identity suggestions | Visible Pro options, disabled | Choose a symbol or create a geometric logo; editable tagline and footer suggestions |
| Conversational assistant | Unavailable | Planned paid assistant |
| Licensed packs | Absent from archive | Separately licensed packs |
| Billing and hosting | No checkout, subscription, hosted account or signing tool | Commercial, hosting and account wiring still to be completed |

Pro is planned as a separate $99/month offer. This package has no checkout or Pro activation, and an old or copied
entitlement leaves it in Free mode. Hosted inference, unlimited support and particular vendor subscriptions are
not included promises. Publication identity and editorial correctness remain Free; brand customization is
the supported Pro distinction.

You may modify the source under MIT. The product distinction comes from supported features, separately
licensed packs, hosted infrastructure and service. It does not restrict your MIT rights.
